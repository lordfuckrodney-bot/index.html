Unforgettable O-Level Days

A tribute to the laughs, exams, late nights, and lifelong friendships created during our Ordinary Level school years.

The Journey

From stepping into high school for the first time as nervous freshmen to walking out as confident O-Level graduates, these years shaped who we are. Through long study sessions, sports days, hidden lunches, and endless banter, we made memories that will last a lifetime.

Year by Year

Senior 1 / Year 1 — The Freshmen Days

New uniforms, oversized bags, and trying to figure out where all the classrooms were. This was the year we all met and formed our first inner circles.

Senior 2 / Year 2 — Finding Our Groove

No longer the youngest! We got comfortable, started cracking jokes in class, got involved in clubs and sports, and spent way too much time at the school canteen.

Senior 3 / Year 3 — Subject Choices & Chaos

Picking our specialized subjects. Reality hit us a bit, but our bonds grew stronger. Unforgettable school trips, inter-house competitions, and legendary pranks.

Senior 4 / Year 4 — The Final Stretch & UNEB/Exams

Late-night night prep, past-paper revisions, stress, and final goodbyes. We conquered O-Levels together as a family!
